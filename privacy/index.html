Caption Privacy Policy

Effective 3 September 2026 Caption is a daily photo competition. This explains what it collects, why, who can see it, and what you can remove. It describes what the app actually does — if something here does not match your experience, that is a bug worth reporting to support@caption-app.com. Caption is operated by Caption LLC.

Who can use Caption

You must be at least 13. We ask your date of birth when you sign up and refuse accounts below 13. We do not knowingly collect anything from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email support@caption-app.com and we will delete it.

What we collect

When you sign up

  • Email address, used to sign in and to contact you about your account
  • A password, which is hashed by our authentication provider — we never see it
  • A username, which is public
  • Your date of birth, used only to check you are 13 or older. It is never shown on your

profile and cannot be changed once set. When you use the app

  • Photos you take. Photos are taken in the app. You choose per photo whether it goes

into the competition or stays in your private scrapbook.

  • Your device's timezone, read from your device settings. It determines which regional

competition you are in. We do not collect your location, GPS coordinates, or IP-based location for this — a timezone is all we use.

  • A display name and profile details, if you add them.

As you use the app

  • Which photos you voted for.
  • Which photos you were shown. This is the part that makes scoring fair: a photo is

ranked by the share of people who saw it and voted for it, not by raw vote count, so we have to record that you saw it.

  • Photos you saved, friend relationships and requests, blocks, lobbies you belong to,

and reports you file.

  • When you last opened your activity feed, so we know what is new to you.

What we do not collect

  • Your location, beyond the timezone described above
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Anything from other apps, or any advertising identifier
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising

Who can see what

  • Your username and display name are public to signed-in users.
  • Your competition photo is shown to other users for voting. While voting is open it

is shown without your name — the competition is blind on purpose. Your name appears alongside it only after voting closes.

  • Your scrapbook photos are private to you unless you submit one to the competition.
  • Your votes are never shown to anyone, including the person whose photo you voted for.
  • Your date of birth and email are never shown to other users.
  • Reports are private. The person you report is not told who reported them.
  • Blocks are private and symmetric. We do not tell someone they were blocked.

One thing to be aware of

Photos are stored in a publicly readable bucket. That means a photo's URL will open for anyone who has it, without signing in. Inside the app, access is controlled — but the link itself is not a secret. Do not submit a photo you would not want a stranger to be able to open if the link were shared.

Automated processing

  • Photos may be scanned automatically for adult content before being shown. A photo the

scanner rejects is hidden without a person reviewing it first. You can contest that by emailing support@caption-app.com.

  • Enough reports from other users will hide a photo automatically, pending review by a

person.

  • Scoring is automated — no human decides who wins a day.

Who else processes your information

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and photo storage.
  • Hugging Face runs the automated content check. When a photo is submitted it is sent

to their inference service, which returns a score for whether it contains sexual content. They receive the photo and nothing else -- no name, no email, no account identifier.

  • Apple or Google distribute the app and handle payments if we ever add any.

We do not share your information with anyone else, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

How long we keep things

  • Photos, profile and social data are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Votes and views are kept after you delete your account, without your identity. They

are detached from you and attached to an anonymous placeholder. This is deliberate: those records are part of other people's scores, and deleting them would retroactively change results and could remove someone else's past win. They cannot be linked back to you.

  • Records of past winners keep the numbers but not the name. If you won a day and then

deleted your account, the day still shows a result; it no longer names you.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account, inside the app. It removes your photos and the image files behind them, your profile and username, saved photos, friendships, lobby memberships, and reports you filed, then deletes the account itself. Your username becomes available again. As described above, votes and views remain in anonymised form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information, or to object to how it is used. Deletion is built into the app. For anything else, email support@caption-app.com and we will respond within 30 days. We do not currently have an automated way to export a copy of your data. If you ask, we will assemble it by hand.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version.
