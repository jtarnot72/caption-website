Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Caption ("we," "us") is a mobile app currently in development. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit this website or join our waitlist.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you submit. We don't collect any other personal information through this site, and we don't use tracking cookies or analytics at this time.

How we use it

Your email is used only to notify you about Caption's launch, TestFlight access, and related updates. We won't sell or share your email with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

Waitlist submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. The site is hosted on Vercel. Review their respective privacy policies for details on how they handle data.

Data retention

We keep your email until Caption launches and related communications are complete, or until you ask us to remove it.

Your rights

You can request that we delete your email from our waitlist at any time by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

As Caption moves from waitlist to a live app, this policy will be updated to reflect the data the app itself collects. We'll post any changes here.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@caption-app.com.
